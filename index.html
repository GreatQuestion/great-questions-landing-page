FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this have access to my data?

No. Everything runs locally on your Mac. Great Questions works through your own Claude, and only reaches the apps you've already connected there. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.

Will it post or change anything in Slack, Linear, or GitHub?

No. The scan only reads. It never posts, comments, reacts, or edits. Anything beyond reading, like drafting a study, only happens when you click a button on a card.

macOS says it can't open the app. What do I do?

That's expected. This prototype isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. To open it:

  1. Open Great Questions from Applications. When macOS blocks it, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message about Great Questions and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this once. If you're comfortable in Terminal, this does the same thing: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Great Questions.app"

What if I haven't connected Slack, Linear, or GitHub?

Great Questions skips any source that isn't connected and uses the rest. To add one, open Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors. It's picked up on the next scan.

When does it look for new questions?

Every weekday at 8am, while the Claude app is open. If Claude was closed, the scan runs the next time you open it. You can also run one any time from the app.
